GAME

Vocabulary Games

Practice vocabulary through Word Search and Hangman.

WORD SEARCH · RULES
  1. Choose one or more Days. Every additional selected Day adds 1% to the new final score.
  2. Choose EASY, NORMAL, or HARD. Higher difficulty uses a larger grid, more target words, and more time.
  3. Read the Korean meaning, then drag from the first letter to the last letter of the matching English word.
  4. Correct finds give points and build a combo. Consecutive finds increase the combo reward.
  5. Find all target words before time runs out to complete the game. A time-out still records the points earned.
  6. The Day bonus is applied only to the new score being saved. Existing ranking scores remain unchanged.
HANGMAN · RULES
  1. Choose one or more Days, then start. One word is randomly selected from the chosen Days.
  2. Guess the English word one letter at a time with the on-screen keyboard.
  3. Wrong guesses increase the mistake count. Solve the word before the mistake limit is reached.
  4. After solving the English word, enter its Korean meaning and press Check.
  5. Correct answers are scored by solving time, so a faster solution earns a higher basic score.
  6. The selected-Day bonus is then applied: each additional selected Day adds 1% to the new score.
